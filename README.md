# Factory_Outlet_Sales
### This Repository has Factory Outlet Sales Analysis – Adidas

## Project Overview
This project presents an end-to-end sales and revenue analysis of Adidas Factory Outlet operations using an interactive business intelligence dashboard.
The objective is to help management understand revenue drivers, regional performance, retailer contribution, product mix, and customer behavior to support data-driven strategic decisions.

The dashboard consolidates key KPIs and provides dynamic filtering by Year, Region, Retailer, Product Category, Gender, Sales Method, and Month.

## Business Objectives
- Track overall revenue, unit sales, and cost metrics
- Identify top-performing retailers, regions, products, and cities
- Understand customer gender contribution
- Analyze monthly and yearly revenue trends
- Support strategic decisions for inventory, pricing, and expansion


## Key Insights
### Retailer Performance

- West Gear ($32.4M) and Foot Locker ($29.0M) are the top revenue-generating retailers.
- Amazon and Walmart contribute comparatively lower revenue, indicating potential underutilization or pricing strategy gaps.


### Regional Analysis

- West region leads revenue contribution (~$36.4M), followed by Northeast.
- Midwest shows the lowest revenue, highlighting an opportunity for targeted growth strategies.
  
### Product Category Insights

- Men’s Street Footwear ($27.7M) is the highest-selling product category.
- Women’s Apparel ($23.9M) and Men’s Athletic Footwear ($20.6M) also perform strongly.
- Women’s Athletic Footwear contributes the least, suggesting scope for marketing or product repositioning.

### Monthly Revenue Trends

- Peak revenue observed in July ($13M) and August ($12M), indicating strong seasonal demand.
- Revenue dips during February and October, useful for planning promotions or inventory clearance.

### Gender-Wise Revenue Contribution

- Women customers contribute 54% of total revenue.
- Men contribute 46%, showing balanced but slightly higher demand from women customers.

### Sales Method Analysis

- Outlet sales (37%) lead overall revenue contribution.
- Online (33%) and In-store (30%) channels show balanced distribution, highlighting a strong omnichannel presence.

### Geographic Hotspots

- New York, San Francisco, and Portland are among the top revenue-generating cities.
- Cities like Orlando show lower revenue, presenting opportunities for localized marketing efforts.


# Business Recommendations

 1. Strengthen High-Performing Retailers

- Deepen partnerships with West Gear and Foot Locker through exclusive launches and volume discounts.
- Reassess strategies with Amazon and Walmart to improve visibility and sales conversion.

 2. Regional Growth Strategy

- Focus expansion and promotional campaigns in Midwest and South regions.
- Replicate successful sales models from the West region.

 3. Product Portfolio Optimization

- Increase inventory and promotions for Men’s Street Footwear and Women’s Apparel.
- Improve demand for Women’s Athletic Footwear through targeted marketing and product redesign.

 4. Seasonal Planning

- Maximize inventory availability before peak months (July–August).
- Introduce discounts and campaigns during low-performing months to stabilize revenue.

 5. Enhance Omnichannel Experience

- Strengthen Outlet + Online integration for better customer reach.
- Use data-driven personalization to increase repeat purchases.


## Conclusion
This dashboard delivers a comprehensive performance overview of Adidas Factory Outlet operations. By leveraging these insights, management can enhance profitability, customer targeting, inventory planning, and regional expansion strategies.
